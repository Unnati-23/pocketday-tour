Pocketday

Your pay, split before you spend. One number a day for everything else. A daily budget and savings app for India.

Try the app See every screen

Every screen here shows an example month. Made-up data, no real bank details.

What it does

On payday, bills and savings come out first. What's left becomes one number you can spend today.

No spreadsheets, no category maths. Open the app, see today's number, and know whether a coffee or a Myntra order still fits.

The daily number

How it's worked out

StepExample month
Pay this cycle₹5,00,000
Bills and EMIs set aside− ₹39,196
Invest (20% of pay)− ₹1,00,000
Save (60% of pay)− ₹3,00,000
Left to live on₹60,804
Divided by 30 days₹2,026 a day

The daily number is rounded down, so it never promises more than you have. You choose the invest and save amounts; the app gives no investment advice.

Screen tour

Every screen, with the example month

Tap any screen to see it bigger. Swipe sideways on a row to see more.

Today

The ring fills as you spend. In the example, Zepto, a café, Myntra and Swiggy add up to ₹1,679 of the ₹2,026 daily number, so ₹347 is left today.

Plan

Bills, invest and save come out first. What's left is split into pockets for everyday life, and anything unspent rolls into a big-buy fund.

Bills and cycles

Rent, EMIs, phone and subscriptions like ChatGPT Plus and Meta Verified are set aside before the daily number. Pause a bill and its money goes back into the day.

Activity

Every payment, refund and transfer, and whether it counted. When the bank sends the same SMS twice, the copy is crossed out and not counted.

Insights

How the last 7 days compare with the daily number, spend per category this cycle, and payments that look like they repeat every month.

Memories and setup

Every purchase becomes a card with the place, time and amount. Quick start loads the example month into your own account so you can try everything.

Trust

Safety and privacy

Your rows are yours

Apper's row rules let each account see and change only its own bills, budgets and payments.

No bank logins

The app never asks for bank passwords, card numbers or access to your messages.

No secrets in the code

Only public project keys ship with the app; anything private stays in Apper Secrets.

No investment advice

You choose what to invest and save. Pocketday keeps it aside and doesn't say where to put it.
