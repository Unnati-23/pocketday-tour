Step 1

Get into the app

Recommended

Make your own account

  1. Open the app link above.
  2. Tap Sign up and use any email and password.
  3. Tap Quick start to load the example month.

Each reviewer gets a private account. Nothing you add is visible to anyone else.

Alternative

Use the reviewer account

If you'd rather not sign up, Unnati will email you a reviewer login separately. Logins are never posted on this page.

Step 2

What to check, in about five minutes

  1. Today's ring

    After Quick start, the Today screen shows the daily number (₹2,026) and how much is left after today's example spends.

  2. Add a payment and watch it update

    Tap +, add a ₹200 expense, save. The ring and "left today" change straight away.

  3. Duplicates aren't counted

    Open Activity. The second Swiggy ₹420 is crossed out as a duplicate SMS and doesn't count toward the day.

  4. Change the plan

    Open Plan, change the Save amount, and the daily number at the bottom recalculates before you save.

  5. Data separation

    Sign up a second account in a private window. It starts empty and can't see the first account's bills or payments.

  6. Start empty

    On the Quick start screen, Start empty removes only what Quick start added.
